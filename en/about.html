About ALYNX Engineering

Engineering and manufacturing company based in the industrial center of Stalowa Wola, Poland.

Who We Are

Passion for Technology and Uncompromising Attention to Detail

ALYNX Engineering Sp. z o.o. is an engineering and manufacturing company based in Stalowa Wola, Poland, established by combining a passion for mechanical technology, industry experience, and strict attention to detail. We specialize in designing and manufacturing complex welded steel structures, complete turnkey production lines, and dedicated industrial tooling.

Our headquarters and fabrication facility are situated in the heart of Stalowa Wola's industrial zone. Operating in a city renowned for heavy engineering traditions gives us robust supply chain infrastructure, logistical connectivity across Europe, and optimal conditions to execute complex contracts for domestic and export clients.

Solid Workshop Infrastructure & Turnkey Capability

Our fabrication plant is equipped with two 5-ton radio-controlled overhead cranes, providing safe and agile handling of heavy, oversized weldments. Assembly and fabrication processes take place on modular 3D clamping tables, ensuring tight geometric tolerances and repeatability. In-house automated shot blasting and airless painting capabilities allow us to apply high-durability coating systems suitable for the harshest working environments.

We do not limit ourselves solely to manufacturing. We provide full-lifecycle technical support across every project phase:

  • In-house engineering & CAD office develops 3D models and 2D workshop documentation, optimizing designs for cost and manufacturability.
  • Large-scale CNC machining & plate cutting (laser, plasma, oxy-fuel) are coordinated through a vetted network of permanent partner facilities, eliminating machine and dimensional bottlenecks.

We deliver solutions for demanding industrial sectors: railway, energy, offshore, defense, construction, and food processing (stainless and acid-resistant alloys).
