About ALYNX Engineering

An engineering and manufacturing company in the heart of Stalowa Wola’s industrial district.

Who We Are

Passion for Technology & Uncompromising Attention to Detail

ALYNX Engineering Sp. z o.o. is an engineering and manufacturing company headquartered in Stalowa Wola, Poland. Formed from deep technological passion and extensive industrial experience, we specialize in the engineering design and fabrication of complex steel structures, complete production lines, and dedicated manufacturing jigs and fixtures.

Our plant is situated in the industrial center of Stalowa Wola. Located in a city with storied industrial heritage dating back to the Central Industrial Region (COP), we enjoy superior logistics infrastructure and an exceptional regional network for executing demanding domestic and international projects.

Reliable Technical Facilities & Integrated Scope

We operate a production hall equipped with two radio-controlled overhead cranes with a capacity of 5 tons each, allowing safe, efficient maneuvering of heavy assemblies. Welding and fitting take place on modular 3D tables ensuring strict geometric compliance. Our own automated shot blasting chambers and airless industrial coating setup ensure resilient surface protection for the harshest working environments.

We do not stop at manufacturing alone. We provide support throughout the entire project lifecycle:

  • In-House Engineering Design Office develops 3D CAD solid models and 2D fabrication prints, optimizing designs for manufacturing efficiency (DFM).
  • Advanced CNC Machining & Plate Cutting (fiber laser, HD plasma, and oxy-fuel) are executed in partnership with our audited network of regional suppliers, eliminating machine and envelope limitations.

We supply precision solutions for: railway, power generation, offshore, defense, industrial construction, and food processing (stainless and acid-resistant steels).
