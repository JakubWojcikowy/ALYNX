About the Company

Engineering & Manufacturing Company in Stalowa Wola

ALYNX Engineering Sp. z o.o. is an engineering and manufacturing company based in Stalowa Wola, Poland, founded on a passion for technology, industry experience, and uncompromising attention to detail. We specialize in designing and fabricating complex welded steel structures, complete production lines, and custom industrial tooling.

Our facility is located in the heart of Stalowa Wola's industrial zone — a city with profound heavy manufacturing heritage. This location provides us with exceptional supply-chain access, logistics connectivity, and ideal conditions to execute demanding contracts for domestic and international clients.

We supply specialized solutions across major industrial sectors: railway, energy, offshore, defense, construction, and food processing (stainless and acid-resistant steel).
