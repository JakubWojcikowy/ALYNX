Airless Spray Painting & Shot Blasting

Large-Scale CNC Machining & Plate Cutting

About the Company

Engineering & Manufacturing Company in Stalowa Wola

ALYNX Engineering Sp. z o.o. is an engineering and manufacturing company based in Stalowa Wola, Poland. We specialize in the design and production of complex steel structures, complete assembly lines, and specialized industrial tooling.

Our plant is situated in the industrial heart of Stalowa Wola. We supply turn-key components for the railway, power generation, offshore, defense, structural construction, and food processing (stainless/acid-resistant) sectors.

Project execution and direct customer relations are managed by Sebastian Ajryś. We welcome collaboration from initial CAD concept drawings to finished, certified products.
